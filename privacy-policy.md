# Privacy Policy for Spider Territory (Spider Wars)

**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026  

This Privacy Policy describes how **Spider Territory: Spider Wars** ("we", "us", or "our", package name: `com.spidergame.rival`) handles your information when you download, install, and play our game. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a safe gaming environment.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information We Do NOT Collect Directly
We do not require you to create an account, register, or provide personal identification details (such as your real name, physical address, phone number, or contacts) to play Spider Territory. 

### B. Game Progress & Local Data
Your gameplay progress, unlocked levels (Bugs Mode and Rival Mode), game scores, power inventory, settings (sound, music), and coin balances are stored **locally on your device** (in local game configuration files). We do not transmit or store this data on external private servers.

### C. Automatically Collected Information via Third-Party Services
To provide advertisements and process optional in-game purchases, the app integrates trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third parties may automatically collect certain device and diagnostic data:
- **Device Information:** Device model, operating system version, screen resolution, and system language.
- **Advertising Identifiers:** Google Advertising ID (GAID) used for personalized or non-personalized advertisements, ad measurement, and fraud prevention.
- **Diagnostics & Performance:** Crash logs, app performance metrics, and anonymous diagnostic information to help us fix bugs and improve game stability.

---

## 2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Spider Territory integrates the following third-party services:

### A. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads)
We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may use device identifiers and cookies to serve relevant ads and measure ad performance according to Google's Privacy Policy.
- **Google Privacy & Terms:** [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- **Google Ad Technologies:** [https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

You can manage or reset your advertising ID and opt out of personalized ads at any time through your Android device settings (**Settings > Google > Ads**).

### B. Google Play In-App Billing
We use Google Play In-App Billing for optional purchases (such as coin packs and the "Remove Ads" option). All transactions and payment details (such as credit card numbers and billing addresses) are handled directly and securely by Google Play. We never receive or store your financial or payment details.

---

## 3. How We Use Information

The information collected by third-party services is used exclusively to:
- Deliver core gameplay features and reward ad mechanics (e.g., earning extra power charges or coins by watching rewarded videos).
- Display advertisements (banners, interstitials, and rewarded ads).
- Process in-app purchases through Google Play.
- Analyze crash reports to fix bugs and improve performance on Android devices.
- Prevent fraud and enforce Google Play Developer policies.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Spider Territory is designed for general audiences and casual game players. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age in your jurisdiction). If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party services through our game without your consent, please contact us immediately at **muhammadfaysal.khan7@gmail.com**, and we will take prompt steps to investigate and ensure appropriate handling.

## 5. Data Security

We care about safeguarding the confidentiality of your information. Because all gameplay data is stored locally on your device and financial transactions are managed solely by Google Play, your sensitive personal data is protected by Android's application sandbox and Google's high-grade security infrastructure.

---

## 6. Your Rights & Choices

- **Ad Personalization:** You can opt out of interest-based ads or reset your Advertising ID via Android Settings (**Settings > Google > Ads > Delete advertising ID** or **Reset advertising ID**).
- **Removing Ads:** You can permanently disable banner and interstitial ads by purchasing the "Remove Ads" in-app purchase option available in the game's Settings menu.
- **Deleting Game Data:** You can delete all locally stored game data and progress at any time by clearing the game's app data or cache in **Android Settings > Apps > Spider Territory > Storage > Clear Data**.
